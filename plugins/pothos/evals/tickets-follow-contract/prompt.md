Spec: appointment reminders
- Owners can opt in to a reminder for each appointment.
- A reminder is sent 24 hours before the appointment starts, by email.
- Owners can cancel a reminder until it is sent.
- Cancelling an appointment cancels its reminder.
- Out of scope: SMS, push notifications, changing the 24-hour lead time.
